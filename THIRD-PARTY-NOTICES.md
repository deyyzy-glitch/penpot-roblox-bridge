## Smoothed corner geometry

The cubic/arc corner geometry in `penpot/corners.js` is adapted from Supercorner's Luau port of figma-squircle. The browser rasterization, restricted surface eligibility and importer integration are specific to Penpot Bridge. No Supercorner runtime is installed in the user's game.

Sources: https://github.com/cameronpcampbell/supercorner (reviewed revision 22ff6f16e060e1d477055c898421b172591ff23e) and https://github.com/phamfoo/figma-squircle
