What the modes mean

Auto follows the normal fidelity rules. Native requests editable objects. Image flattens the visual subtree. Layout only keeps child content without its own appearance. Skip omits the subtree. Reusable template uses a component mapped in Studio.

Choices are saved locally per design file. Prepare again after making changes. Native mode reports unsupported effects instead of silently dropping them.
